FE-Schrift sdroxide (FESchrift-sdroxide.otf)

Built by tools/gen_fe_font.py from the drawing "File:FE-Schrift.svg" on
Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FE-Schrift.svg):
the glyphs of the FE-Schrift, the typeface of German vehicle number plates,
designed by Karlgeorg Hoefer for the Bundesanstalt für Straßenwesen, and
vectorised by Liftarn. Wikimedia Commons marks that drawing as public domain
("The depicted text is ineligible for copyright and therefore in the public
domain").

This font file is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
to the extent possible under law, the sdroxide authors have waived all
copyright and related or neighbouring rights to it.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

It holds A–Z (lower case maps onto the capitals), 0–9, Ä Ö Ü, a space, a
hyphen and a full stop. Any other character is drawn by sdroxide from its
default face.
